Quick Facts

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you want to know about Yertea's caffeine, ingredients, glass bottles, and wholesale availability.

How much caffeine is in a bottle of Yertea? +

Each 12 fl oz glass bottle of Yertea contains 150mg of naturally occurring caffeine brewed directly from real yerba mate leaves, paired with natural theobromine for smooth, sustained focus without a synthetic energy crash.

What ingredients are in Yertea? +

Yertea is brewed hot from real yerba mate leaves and blended with real fruit juice (fresh-pressed lemon or red raspberry puree), pure cane sugar, raw wildflower honey, and citric acid. It contains zero synthetic taurine, artificial sweeteners, or lab-made stimulants.

How is Yertea packaged and sold? +

Yertea is bottled in classic 12oz long-neck glass bottles and is available online in Single Bottles ($3.89) or Six-Packs ($22.50) across three flavors: Classic, Lemon Honey, and Raspberry.

Yes. Yertea supplies retail wholesale cases, cafe and bar bottle programs, and event tasting drops. Venue owners can request wholesale pricing, nutritional spec sheets, and sample drops directly through the Wholesale inquiry form below.
